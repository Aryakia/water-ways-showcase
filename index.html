Water Ways

Interactive research infrastructure for mapping System Dynamics and systems-thinking water researchers to the river basins, aquifers, and water systems they actually study.

Water SystemsSystem DynamicsGISResearch InfrastructureArya Kia
The problem

Affiliation is not research geography.

Research directories commonly plot researchers at their universities. For water research, that can be misleading: the system of interest may be a river basin or aquifer hundreds or thousands of kilometres away.

Water Ways separates researcher identity, studied water systems, and institutional affiliation so that each represents a different kind of information.

The approach

Map the system being studied.

A researcher may study several basins or aquifers, and the same water system may connect many researchers. The platform preserves those many-to-many relationships and distinguishes source-named geography from current GIS matches.

What I built

Research infrastructure

Researcher directory

Profiles, affiliations, methods, source context, and links are preserved alongside the geographic research record.

Basin & aquifer layers

Global basin context and transboundary aquifer geography provide a spatial framework for research discovery.

Evidence-aware mapping

Uncertain or unmatched system names remain visible rather than being forced into unsupported geographic matches.

Search & filters

Explore researchers, water systems, methods, and locations through a searchable interactive interface.

Layer controls

Research geography and contextual layers can be inspected independently rather than collapsed into one map.

Reusable data

Filtered research-geography records can be exported for analysis and further community development.

Repository boundary

Public showcase, private working source.

This repository presents the project and its design logic. The canonical working application, research-processing workflow, and internal development materials remain in a separate private repository.
